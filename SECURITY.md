# Security & Ethics

This repository is educational material for **building and defending** AI systems.

- **Toy targets only.** Every attack here runs against this repo's own deliberately-vulnerable sample systems. Nothing here is a weaponized payload against a real product, model, or service.
- **Every attack ships its defense.** No lab teaches an exploit without the mitigation in the same folder. The point is defense; the attack exists to show why the defense matters.
- **No live credentials, no real targets.** Accounts, keys, and services in these labs are fictional placeholders.
- **Responsible use.** Use these techniques only on systems you own or are explicitly authorized to test. Applying them to systems you do not control may be illegal.

Found an issue in the lab code itself? Open an issue or reach out before publicizing details.
